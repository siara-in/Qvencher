Information Collection And Use

The Applications DO NOT transmit personal information or co-ordinates (location data) to the Author, his affiliates or his servers. The Author has no user accounts and does not receive your files, queries or data. The only information the Applications may send, through the third-party services described under "Analytics and crash reporting" and "Advertising" below, is technical and usage information about the app itself.

Data stored on your device

Files you open, queries you run and the results you view are processed locally on your device. Settings, query history, spreadsheets and any files downloaded by the Application are stored on your device only, and are removed when you delete the Application or its data. They are never sent to the Author.

Network features

Some features connect to the internet only when you use them. For example, "Open from web" sends the text you search for to dataset services such as Hugging Face and open-data portals (data.gov.uk, open.canada.ca, data.humdata.org), and downloads the data files you choose from the servers hosting them. Data sources you connect to (databases, cloud services) receive the credentials and queries you provide. These services will see your IP address and the request details, and handle them under their own privacy policies. The Author does not receive this information.

Analytics and crash reporting

The Applications may use third-party services such as Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous or pseudonymous technical and usage information: for example crash reports and error logs, app version, device model, operating system version, language, general (country-level) location derived from the IP address, which features are used, and a randomly generated app-instance identifier. This information is used only to find and fix problems, understand which features are useful and improve the Applications. It does not include your files, queries, query results or the contents of your data, the Author does not use it to identify or contact you, and it is not sold or used for advertising by the Author. The information is processed by Google under Firebase's privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law, this collection is subject to your consent, and you can turn it off where the Application offers such a setting or by resetting or limiting your device's advertising and analytics permissions.
